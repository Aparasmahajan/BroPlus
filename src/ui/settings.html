Privacy and security

This build has neither screen-share hiding nor ad blocking (see Plugins in this build).

Hidden from screen share

Hides this window from software screen capture (Teams, Meet, Zoom, OBS, screen recorders). It does not hide from a phone camera or an HDMI capture card. Only record or share meetings where you have consent.

Profiles

Each profile is a separate browser: its own sign-ins and cookies, history, bookmarks and extensions. The profile button (top right) opens a tab in another profile; tabs show their profile's colour. Links you open from a tab stay in its profile.

Open magnet links and .torrent files in Bro Torrents download into the folder above and stop when they finish (no seeding). Turn this off to hand them to your torrent app instead.

Save for offline (pages + videos)

Video quality Ctrl/Cmd+S (or the Save-for-offline button in the toolbar) saves the page and its video into “Bro Offline” in the folder above. A 1-hour lecture is roughly 200 MB at 360p, 500 MB at 720p, 40 MB as audio only.
Save English subtitles When the video has them. They show in the offline player.
Video tools yt-dlp finds the video on thousands of sites; ffmpeg turns any format into one that plays. About 50 MB, fetched once.

Search engine

Search engine used in the address bar Used when you type words that aren't a web address.

Keywords: type a keyword, a space, then your search in the address bar or on the new-tab page, e.g. yt lofi or w Electron. In the URL, %s marks where the search terms go.

Keyboard shortcuts

Click + next to a command, then press the keys you want (include Ctrl, Alt or CmdWin; F-keys work alone). Esc cancels. A key can only do one thing — assigning it moves it from its old command. System-wide groups work even when Bro isn't focused.

Plugins in this build

Features are plugins chosen when Bro Plus is built (plugins.config.json or the BRO_PLUGINS variable). A plugin left out of the build has no button, no settings and no code here.

Your data

Back up or move Bro One .json file with bookmarks, history, sessions (incl. the tabs open now), shortcuts, search engines and startup choice. Cookies and sign-ins are not included.

Clear browsing data Cookies sign you out of websites. Cache is just downloaded files and is safe to clear.
